The Machine

Brass, walnut, and one gear that drives nothing

I built it in the spring and I still do not know what it is for. It has one lever, and one slot where a small card comes out. Pull the lever and it will do something. It will not, however, do anything useful.

Some things are worth making for no reason at all. This is one of them.

Model IV No. 4

Something will come out.

The one good thing it does

Clearing the path

There is a gate at one end of the yard and a tree at the other, and every time it snows the path between them is buried. The machine is good at exactly this and nothing else.

You get sixteen strokes of the shovel. The snow does not wait for you to finish.

Strokes left: 16 Clear a path from the gate to the tree.

Drag across the snow to shovel. Every tile you clear costs one stroke, and one tile of snow falls again straight after. Open the path from the gate to the tree before the strokes run out.
